Deadline Extension - October 31, 2025

Per Revenue Memorandum Circular No. 88-2025

BIR extends the deadlines for Filing, Payment of Taxes, and Submission of Documents

For taxpayers and BIR Offices, including their Authorized Agent Banks, affected by the recent Cebu earthquake:

1. RDO No. 80 - Mandaue City, Cebu

2. RDO No. 81 - Cebu City North

3. RDO No. 82 - Cebu City South

4. RDO No. 83 - Talisay City, Cebu

5. RDO No. 123 - Large Taxpayers Division - Cebu

Tax Amnesty Program Extended

Per Revenue Memorandum Circular No. 92-2025

Take advantage of the extended Tax Amnesty Program until December 31, 2025

Benefits include:

✓ Waiver of penalties and surcharges

✓ No criminal prosecution

✓ Simplified filing requirements

✓ Affordable amnesty tax rates
